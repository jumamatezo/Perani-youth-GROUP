About Perani Self Help Group

Our Story, Mission, and Impact

Our Story

Perani Self Help Group was founded with a vision to create positive change in our community. Starting as a small group of dedicated youth, we have grown into a vibrant organization that touches thousands of lives.

Our journey has been marked by challenges overcome, successes celebrated, and most importantly, lives transformed through collective action and mutual support.

Today, we continue to expand our reach and impact, staying true to our core values of unity, integrity, and social responsibility.

Our Foundation

🎯 Mission

To empower youth through education, skills development, and community engagement, fostering personal growth and social responsibility.

🌍 Vision

A thriving, inclusive community where every young person has the opportunity to reach their full potential and contribute meaningfully to society.

💎 Values

Unity: Together we are stronger
Integrity: Honest and ethical conduct
Excellence: Commitment to quality
